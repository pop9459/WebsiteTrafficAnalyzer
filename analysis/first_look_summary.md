- 298,296 requests over 86 days (3,474 per day), plus 44,530 error-log lines.

- 81.2% of all requests end in 4xx; 18.7% in 2xx.
- 3,143 requests (1.1%) are not valid HTTP (e.g. TLS handshakes on port 80).
- Outgoing response bytes: 9.51 GB in total, of which 9.07 GB (95%) answered probes for exposed files (mostly `/.git/objects/`).
